## 2. Salt brand (with Isidro) — Los Roques sea salt as premium electrolytes

### Basics
- Partners: me + **Isidro**. Based in Venezuela, salt from **Los Roques**, **extracted by hand**, all handmade.
- Positioning: **premium/VIP electrolyte salt** for wealthy buyers. Main market: wealthy Venezuelans. Target price **~$100 per jar**.
- Packaging must be **glass** and feel really premium.
- Possible later: a seasoning line (partners not told yet — keep it private).

### Research — how the best do it
- **Jacobsen Salt Co.** (Oregon): clear glass, minimal blue/white label; "every flake inspected and packaged by hand"; place = brand.
- **Saltverk** (Iceland): hand-harvested with geothermal heat; used by Noma and Michelin chefs; jars + 4-jar gift box (≤ $45).
- **Halen Môn** (Wales): ceramic clamp-top jar + wooden scoop, reusable; ~$70 per 100 g via import resellers.
- **Sal de Ibiza**: ceramic pot + spoon, ~€25 / 150 g.
- **Guérande fleur de sel**: linen bags and cork-topped crocks.
- **Maldon**: carton box with a consistent identity for decades.
- Electrolyte brands (LMNT, SALT!) use stick packs — functional, not luxury. **Nobody owns "luxury electrolyte salt" → open lane.**
- Pattern: keepable vessel + included scoop + restrained label where the salt is the hero + real proof of craft.

### Recommended packaging (to justify $100)
1. **Jar:** heavy, thick-walled extra-clear glass (luxury-candle-jar weight). **Handmade wooden lid by a local Venezuelan carpenter** with a **silicone gasket** (salt is hygroscopic; wood alone isn't airtight). **No bare metal lids or brass spoons** — salt corrodes them.
2. **Dosing spoon** (wood or ceramic) = 1 serving → sells it as an electrolyte, not cooking salt. Lab-test the mineral profile (Na, Mg, K, Ca) and print real numbers. ⚠️ Sea salt is mostly NaCl — if Mg/K are low, the "electrolyte" claim is weak; check before finalising.
3. **Label:** cotton paper, letterpress or hot foil, **hand-numbered**, harvest date, harvester's name. **Wax seal / paper band** over the lid.
4. **Box:** rigid magnetic box with fitted insert; card with a Los Roques map marking the harvest site + the daily ritual.
5. **Refills** in linen/cotton pouches (Guérande style) → keep the $100 jar, recurring revenue.

### Constraints
- Local glass is unreliable: Venvidrio (ex-Owens-Illinois, expropriated 2010) was ~90% idle by 2017. Import jars (Colombia/Peldar, Mexico, Spain/Italy — Estal, Verallia — or China). Sample first.
- Decorating glass (print/etch/frost) usually needs 10k–50k units; custom molds ~100k units and 16+ weeks. **Start with a stock premium jar**; put the luxury into lid, label, seal and box.
- **Registro sanitario** via SACS (Ministry of Health). Spanish label pre-approved: product name, ingredients, net content, batch, manufacture/expiry dates, storage, nutrition table, instructions. Estimated 6–12 months total → start now, in parallel with design.

## 5. AI production playbook (lessons learned — follow these)

### Tools & costs (as of Oct 2026)
- **Higgsfield** (Starter plan): images. `nano_banana_2_1` edits ≈ 1.5 credits; `gpt_image_2_5` ≈ 0.25; upscale ≈ 2; outpaint ≈ 2. Voice clone TTS (`seed_audio` with an audio reference) ≈ 0.7/line. Kling Pro on Higgsfield needs a higher plan. Credit packs: 100 cr / $6, 200 cr / $12.
- **fal**: videos. `kling/v4/image-to-video` ≈ $0.157/s, 3–30 s, 1080p, native 24 fps, supports start + end frame. Lip sync `fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v3` ≈ $8/min (looked natural). Veo is retired on fal. Zonos voice clone sounded robotic — don't use.

### Rules
- **Verify every still before animating.** Don't animate a frame I haven't approved.
- **Drone shots:** give Kling a start frame + an end frame that's a crop of the start frame at the target (same angle) → it flies straight with no curve.
- **Never slow clips down with `setpts`** — it makes them look like 10 fps. Generate the clip at the native length you need instead.
- AI clips are 24 fps; for a 30 fps timeline, motion-interpolate them (`minterpolate=fps=30:mi_mode=mci`) to avoid judder.
- **Audio mixing:** place each VO line at exact sample offsets (numpy) or plain `adelay` + `amix normalize=0`, then `loudnorm`. A chain with `apad`/`atrim` produced a garbled "1000 voices" mix. Always transcribe the final mix (faster-whisper) to verify.
- Cloned TTS mimics the reference loudness (very quiet) → boost + loudnorm. Write brand words phonetically if TTS drops them ("Cóconat Uóter").
- Generate names as one phrase and split by word timestamps rather than single-word generations.
- **Image edits degrade:** every AI re-edit adds blur. For small additions (props, text), composite locally onto the sharp original instead of re-generating.
- Reel covers: keep text + key subjects inside the centre 3:4 so the profile grid crop doesn't cut them.
- Check scale: animals at realistic size vs a small handheld bottle; bottle on **dry** sand, not in water.
